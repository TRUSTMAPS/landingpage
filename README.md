# TRUSTMAPS

National Center for Measurement and Assurance of Trustworthiness, Privacy and Security of Medical AI Systems.

## Overview

TRUSTMAPS is a national research initiative focused on building the evaluation and certification infrastructure needed to make medical AI trustworthy, privacy-preserving, secure, fair, and clinically useful for India.

The center develops methods to assess AI systems beyond accuracy. Its work includes:

- security and privacy testing
- fairness and equity audits
- explainability analysis
- uncertainty and abstention evaluation
- certification frameworks and trust labels

## Mission

The goal of TRUSTMAPS is to create a national ecosystem for responsible medical AI deployment. The center aims to support hospitals, regulators, researchers, and industry partners with evidence-based tools for measuring and assuring trustworthiness.

## Vision

TRUSTMAPS envisions a future where medical AI systems used in India can be evaluated consistently, certified responsibly, and deployed safely at scale.
